4 · The real build

How I'd build yours

This page is the core of it, working. Here is how it becomes your chatbot, on your stack.

1

Your PDFs in, page by page

Python: PDF text extraction and chunking that keeps the file and page, embeddings in pgvector inside your PostgreSQL, so there is no extra database to run. We write down 20 or so of your users' real questions, including ones it must refuse.

2

A FastAPI endpoint, an LLM behind the guard

An async FastAPI route your React chat calls. OpenAI or Claude writes each answer from the retrieved pages only and cites them; the guard you see here stays in code and checks every answer.

3

Measured, then handed over

The question set runs before and after every change and you get the count, the way this page shows 7 of 12 becoming 12 of 12. Modular, documented, with the tests in the repo.

For your developer: the 12-question run and the guard

Opening this runs questions.py in your browser. Standard library Python; BM25 retrieval over eight page chunks stands in for embeddings in pgvector here.
