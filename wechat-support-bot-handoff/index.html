4 · The real build

How I'd build yours

This page is the hard part, working. Here is how it becomes your full chatbot.

1

Your FAQs and documents in

Your FAQs, help articles and product documents chunked and embedded into Supabase (or Pinecone/Qdrant), with simple instructions for adding more. We write down 30 or so of your customers' real questions, including ones it must answer "I'm not sure".

2

Your channels and your team

Website widget or messaging app in, retrieval from the knowledge base, the OpenAI or Claude reply behind the same guard, and the handoff to Slack, email or your helpdesk with the full chat. Editable prompts and escalation rules.

3

Measured, then documented

The question set runs before and after every change and you get the count, the way this page shows 11 of 12 becoming 12 of 12. Then a short Loom walkthrough so your team can update the knowledge base without me.

planned shape (built on the contract)
  Webhook (website widget) / messaging app trigger
  -> wants_human(message)?            yes -> Slack/email handoff with full chat -> reply "handing you to a person"
  -> vector store (Supabase, Pinecone or Qdrant): top passages
  -> IF score below threshold or a name missing from the passage -> "I'm not sure" + handoff
  -> OpenAI / Claude call (system prompt: answer only from these passages, name the source)
  -> every sentence found in the passages?  no -> "I'm not sure" + handoff
  -> log name, email, issue type and conversation (a sheet or your CRM)  ·  on any error -> alert
