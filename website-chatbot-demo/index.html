4 · The real build

How I'd build yours

This page is the hard part, working. Here is how it becomes your full chatbot.

1

Your company information in

The information you provide plus your website pages, chunked and embedded with the OpenAI API. We write down 30 or so of your customers' real questions, including ones it must answer "not available" (and hand to your team).

2

Into your website

A /api/chat route on your server (Node.js, Python or PHP) calls OpenAI or Claude with the retrieved passages only, behind the same guard, and a clean, responsive chat widget on your site. Your key stays on the server.

3

Measured, then documented

The question set runs before and after every change and you get the count, the way this page shows 8 of 12 becoming 12 of 12. Then a short guide, so your team can update the company information without me.
