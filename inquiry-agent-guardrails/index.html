    4 · The real build

    How this becomes your system in 10 working days

    This page is the first slice. Here is how it becomes the automated sales and marketing system in your post.

    1

    Days 1–5: the inquiry agent on your site

    We write your fact sheet together: what it may answer, and what always comes to you. It goes live on your site chat and inbox, with your approval queue.

    2

    Days 6–10: leads, uploads and emails

    Every inquiry saved to your CRM or sheet with no duplicates. Follow-up emails that respect unsubscribes. Then handover and a walkthrough.

    3

    After that: it learns from your answers

    Each question you answer can go into the fact sheet, so your queue shrinks week by week. A test set of real inquiries is re-run before every change.

    For your developer: the run on all 12 inquiries

    The same pipeline, logged. In the real build a model writes the drafts. The dedupe, the do-not-email list and the approval routing stay as plain code in front of it, so a model mistake cannot send a promise you did not approve.
