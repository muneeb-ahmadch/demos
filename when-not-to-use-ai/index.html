How it decides

codePrices, seat limits and trial dates are computed in Python. A model never does arithmetic on your customer's bill.

documentsPolicy questions are answered from the retrieved help-centre page, and the answer names its source.

declinedIf retrieval finds nothing strong enough, it says so and flags it for a person, instead of guessing.

One help-centre page has an instruction hidden in it. The assistant drops that sentence before answering.
