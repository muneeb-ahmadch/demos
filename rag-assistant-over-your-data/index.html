4 · The real build

How I'd build yours

This page is the hard part, working. Here is how it becomes your full chatbot.

1

Your PDFs, documents and website in

Python and LangChain: ingestion and chunking for your files and pages, embeddings in a vector database (Chroma, pgvector or Pinecone). We write down 30 or so of your customers' real questions, including ones it must answer "not available".

2

The LLM behind the guard

OpenAI or Claude writes each answer from the retrieved passages only and names them. The guard you see here stays in code and checks every answer.

3

Measured, then documented

The question set runs before and after every change and you get the count, the way this page shows 6 of 12 becoming 12 of 12. Then the basic setup documentation, so you can add documents yourself.
