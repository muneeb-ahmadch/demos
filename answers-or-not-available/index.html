4 · The real build

How I'd build yours

This page is the hard part, working. Here is how it becomes your full chatbot.

1

Your sources in, your questions written down

Ingestion and chunking for your documents, website and database, embeddings in pgvector (or Pinecone or Qdrant). We agree 30 or so real customer questions, including ones it must answer "not available", as the acceptance test.

2

The model behind the guard

OpenAI or Claude writes the answer from the retrieved passages only and cites them. The guard you see here stays in code and runs on every answer. A Laravel or Node backend with streaming.

3

The rest of your list, shipped

React chat with history, the admin panel for uploads, conversation logs and basic analytics, deployment and docs. The question set runs before every release, and you get the score.
